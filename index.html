How to play

PC — WASD move · mouse aim · LMB fire · RMB aim · Space jump (twice at a low wall = climb) · Shift sprint · C crouch (x2 = prone) · R reload · G / 0 grenade · X switch grenade type · E enter / exit vehicle & loot · Q cycle weapon · I inventory · K bandage · L scoreboard · M map · Esc pause.

MOBILE — left thumb moves (push far = sprint) · right thumb looks · fire buttons shoot · stance tap = crouch, double tap = prone · tap the weapon bar to swap.

VEHICLES — Tank seats 2 (driver + gunner). Helicopter seats 5 (pilot + 2 door gunners + 2 passengers). Walk up and press E. Teammates will board a friendly stationary vehicle on their own.

TEAM DEATHMATCH — every kill your team scores adds 1 to your team score. First team to the score limit, or the highest score when time runs out, wins.

LOOT — every dead soldier drops a crate holding their exact weapons. Open it and swap weapon-slot by weapon-slot. Bots loot each other too.
